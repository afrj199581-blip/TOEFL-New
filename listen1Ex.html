TOEFL Book 2 - Listening Comprehension (1)

Part A: Short Sentences (الجمل القصيرة)
في هذا الجزء، ستستمع إلى جملة قصيرة تُقال مرة واحدة فقط، وعليك اختيار الجملة الأقرب لها في المعنى من بين الخيارات الأربعة.
1.
"When's your flight?"
الإجابة: (C) What time's your departure?
الشرح: المتحدث يسأل عن موعد الرحلة (Flight)، وهذا يطابق تماماً السؤال عن موعد المغادرة (Departure).
2.
"The room was bigger than necessary."
الإجابة: (A) The room could've been smaller.
الشرح: الغرفة كانت "أكبر من اللازم" (bigger than necessary)، مما يعني أنه كان من الممكن أن تكون أصغر (could've been smaller).
3.
"The teacher said that the student should read one chapter per week."
الإجابة: (B) The teacher recommended reading one chapter per week.
الشرح: المعلم "قال إنه يجب" (said that... should)، وهذا يعادل تقديم "توصية" (recommended) بقراءة فصل أسبوعياً.
4.
"Bob told us that the library opened at 10."
الإجابة: (D) He said that we could get into the library at 10:00.
الشرح: بوب أخبرهم أن المكتبة "تفتح الساعة 10"، وهو ما يعني أنهم يستطيعون "الدخول" (get into) في ذلك الوقت.
5.
"I made Sally finish the assignment."
الإجابة: (C) Sally was forced to finish the assignment.
الشرح: التعبير "I made Sally" يعني "أجبرت سالي"، وهو يطابق المعنى المبني للمجهول "was forced" (أُجبرت).
6.
"After a student finishes the required courses, he can take three electives."
الإجابة: (B) A student choose three optional courses after finishing the required courses.
الشرح: بعد إنهاء المواد "الإجبارية" (required)، يمكن للطالب اختيار 3 مواد "اختيارية" (electives = optional courses).
7.
"Martha, who's a marine biologist, led a seminar on the migration of whales."
الإجابة: (A) Martha discussed whale migration.
الشرح: مارثا "قادت ندوة" (led a seminar) عن هجرة الحيتان، مما يعني أنها "ناقشت" (discussed) هذا الموضوع.
8.
"Under no circumstances does the dean listen to any complaints."
الإجابة: (D) The dean never hears any complaints.
الشرح: تعبير "Under no circumstances" للنفي القاطع، ويعني أن العميد "لا يستمع أبداً" (never hears) للشكاوى.
9.
"Mark studied thoroughly for the exam, and Amy did too."
الإجابة: (C) Both Amy and Mark studied hard.
الشرح: مارك درس بجد "وأيضاً إيمي" (Amy did too)، مما يعني أن "كلاهما" (Both) درسا بجد.
10.
"The building might be started next month."
الإجابة: (D) The building probably will begin in a month.
الشرح: "قد يبدأ" (might be started) الشهر القادم، تعادل "من المحتمل أن يبدأ" (probably will begin).
11.
"Well, don't you think this lunch is delicious?"
الإجابة: (C) This lunch really tastes good.
الشرح: المتحدث يعبر عن إعجابه بالغداء بوصفه "لذيذ" (delicious)، مما يعني أن طعمه جيد جداً (tastes good).
12.
"He should check off the names of the students who have completed the assignment."
الإجابة: (C) He should indicate who has finished the work.
الشرح: "وضع علامة" (check off) بجوار أسماء من أكملوا الواجب، تعني "تحديد" (indicate) من أنهى العمل.
13.
"We haven't seen rain like this for many years."
الإجابة: (A) It rained unusually hard this year.
الشرح: "لم نرَ مطراً هكذا منذ سنوات" تعني أنه كان مطراً "غزيراً بشكل غير معتاد" (unusually hard).
14.
"In the nick of time she swerved and missed the truck."
الإجابة: (C) She barely avoided the truck.
الشرح: "في اللحظة الأخيرة" (in the nick of time) انحرفت وتفادت الشاحنة، أي أنها "بالكاد تجنبت" (barely avoided) الشاحنة.
15.
"The lawyer sat her down and broke the news."
الإجابة: (D) The lawyer told her what had happened.
الشرح: التعبير "broke the news" يعني "أخبرها بالأخبار/بما حدث".
16.
"Suzanne bought a stunning new dress for the party."
الإجابة: (D) Suzanne purchased an attractive new outfit.
الشرح: اشترت فستاناً "مذهلاً" (stunning)، وهو ما يعادل شراء زي "جذاب" (attractive new outfit).
17.
"For an additional fee, the agency will add another city to the tour."
الإجابة: (A) It'll cost more to include an additional stop.
الشرح: "مقابل رسوم إضافية" (additional fee) سيضيفون مدينة، مما يعني أن إضافة محطة أخرى "سيكلف أكثر" (cost more).
18.
"The child ran out of the room, too frightened to stay any longer."
الإجابة: (A) The child was too afraid to remain.
الشرح: الطفل ركض لأنه "خائف جداً من البقاء" (too frightened to stay)، وهو نفس معنى "too afraid to remain".
19.
"There's a wide selection of shoes at the mall."
الإجابة: (B) There's a variety of shoes to choose from at the shopping center.
الشرح: "تشكيلة واسعة" (wide selection) في المركز التجاري (mall) تعني "تنوع" (variety) في (shopping center).
20.
"He's yet to attend a seminar."
الإجابة: (C) He hasn't attended any seminars.
الشرح: تعبير "yet to" يفيد النفي، ويعني أنه "لم يحضر أي ندوة حتى الآن" (hasn't attended).
Part B: Short Conversations (المحادثات القصيرة)
21.
- Can we sit anywhere?
- No, this section is reserved.
الإجابة: (C) Some of the seats are being saved for others.
الشرح: كلمة "reserved" (محجوز) تعني أن المقاعد "مُدخرة لآخرين" (saved for others).
22.
- I'm ready for the therapy session. Would you mind taking a seat?
الإجابة: (B) Sit down.
الشرح: المرأة تطلب منه "taking a seat" وهو تعبير مهذب لقول "Sit down" (اجلس).
23.
- Have you finished washing the dishes?
- I've been working on my term paper instead.
الإجابة: (D) The dishes aren't done yet.
الشرح: بما أنه كان منشغلاً بالبحث "بدلاً من ذلك" (instead)، فهذا يعني أن الأطباق لم تُغسل بعد.
24.
- I'm leaving for New York tomorrow at noon. Could you take me to the airport?
- Sorry, I'm working then. Why not see if Mike can help you out?
الإجابة: (C) That she ask someone else to take her.
الشرح: الرجل اقترح عليها أن "تطلب من شخص آخر" (مايك) توصيلها.
25.
- How can I finish all these problems before midnight?
- Why not leave some for the morning?
الإجابة: (D) He shouldn't try to finish everything tonight.
الشرح: نصيحتها بترك بعض الأعمال للصباح تعني أنه "لا ينبغي أن يحاول إنهاء كل شيء الليلة".
26.
- Can you turn off the lights when you leave?
- No problem. Our utility bill is high enough as it is.
الإجابة: (A) The size of the electric bill.
الشرح: الحديث عن الأنوار و"فاتورة الخدمات" (utility bill) يعني أنهم يناقشون "حجم فاتورة الكهرباء".
27.
- Have you seen the announcement in the lobby?
- What announcement is that?
الإجابة: (D) He doesn't know what she's referring to.
الشرح: سؤاله "أي إعلان؟" يعني أنه "لا يعرف عما تتحدث".
28.
- Why don't we shut down for tonight? It's late.
- Shut down? But we have so much more to do.
الإجابة: (A) They haven't finished their work.
الشرح: قولها "لدينا الكثير لنفعله" يعني أنهم "لم ينهوا عملهم بعد".
29.
- Is the lecture tonight worth watching?
- Without a doubt.
الإجابة: (D) She believes the talk will be valuable.
الشرح: قولها "بلا شك" يعني أنها تعتقد أن المحاضرة ستكون "قيّمة" (valuable).
30.
- The anthem just finished and the game's about to start.
- Let's get drinks later then.
الإجابة: (B) Watch the game for now.
الشرح: اقتراحه بتأجيل المشروبات يعني أنه يفضل "مشاهدة المباراة الآن".
31.
- You think we should bring the camera with us?
- That doesn't sound like a bad idea.
الإجابة: (D) He'd like to take some pictures.
الشرح: قوله إن إحضار الكاميرا فكرة غير سيئة يعني أنه "يرغب في التقاط بعض الصور".
32.
- The prices on this airline are a little high, don't you think?
- They seem reasonable for a trip to the moon.
الإجابة: (A) She and the man are in agreement.
الشرح: المرأة تستخدم السخرية (Sarcasm)، فبقولها إن الأسعار معقولة "لرحلة للقمر" هي في الواقع "تتفق معه" أن الأسعار خيالية.
33.
- It never occurred to me that you were an athlete.
- Most people who meet me don't think so either.
الإجابة: (A) He doesn't look like he participates in sports.
الشرح: السؤال يسأل عن الشخص الرياضي الذي تم توجيه الكلام له، والذي يبدو أنه لا يملك بنية رياضية واضحة للناس.
34.
- Can we meet later to work on our presentation?
- How about noon?
الإجابة: (B) She suggests working on the project at 12:00.
الشرح: اقتراح العمل وقت الظهيرة "noon" يطابق تماماً "12:00".
35.
- Why don't we spend our vacation in the Bahamas?
- I don't make enough to do that.
الإجابة: (C) He can't afford the trip.
الشرح: لا يجني ما يكفي من المال (لا يملك الراتب الكافي)، أي أنه "لا يستطيع تحمل تكلفة الرحلة".
Part C: Short Talks (المحادثات الطويلة)
المحادثة الأولى: حوار بين دورا وصديقها حول ورقة بحثية في التاريخ (أسئلة 36-40)
- Dora, could you give me some help? With what? I kept putting off my History 101 paper and it's due next week.
- If you want to pass the course, you've got to write that paper.
- I know. I thought that since you're a history major, you could help me come up with a topic for my paper. History 101 is about American history.
- You could write about the Revolutionary War or the Civil War or World War I.
- Oh, I don't want to write about wars. I don't want to think about killing and death. Can you think of something else?
- Why don't you write about technology, inventions that changed American history?
- That topic seems a little broad. Maybe I should narrow it down a bit.
- Well, you could choose one invention, the telephone or the airplane, for example, and write about its effect on history.
- I know. My favorite topic is cars. I'll write about the invention of the automobile and its effect on American history.
- That sounds like a good topic for you. Now you'd better get busy. You only have one week.
36. Q: What does the man ask the woman to do?
الإجابة: (B) To help him decide on a paper topic.
الشرح: الرجل يطلب منها مساعدته في اختيار "موضوع للبحث".
37. Q: When in the semester does this conversation probably take place?
الإجابة: (C) Near the end of the semester.
الشرح: موعد تسليم البحث في الأسبوع القادم، مما يشير إلى اقتراب نهاية الفصل الدراسي.
38. Q: What course is the man taking this semester?
الإجابة: (D) American History.
الشرح: ذُكر في المحادثة بوضوح أن مقرر History 101 هو عن "التاريخ الأمريكي".
39. Q: Why won't the man choose technology for a topic?
الإجابة: (A) The topic's too general.
الشرح: رفض الرجل موضوع التكنولوجيا ككل لأنه واسع وعام جداً (broad).
40. Q: How much time does the man have to write the paper?
الإجابة: (C) Seven days.
الشرح: ذُكر أن أمامه "أسبوع واحد فقط" (one week)، وهو ما يعادل 7 أيام.
المحادثة الثانية: محاضرة عن تطور الراديو (أسئلة 41-44)
The development of the radio into a worldwide force occurred relatively quickly. In 1920, only 19 years after Marconi sent the first wireless signal across the Atlantic, the world's first radio station was established in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and by 1923, nationwide broadcasting was possible in the United States. Radio broadcasting was initially totally uncontrolled, and each of the dozens of existing stations broadcasted its programs whenever and on whatever wavelength it wanted. The result for listeners, as you can imagine, was often a garbled mess. This confused situation in radio broadcasting lasted until the Federal Communications Commission, which is often referred to as the FCC, was created in 1930 by the United States government. The initial purpose of the FCC was to regulate radio broadcasting. Each station was assigned a wavelength for its broadcasts to minimize interference from other radio stations.
41. Q: What is the topic of this talk?
الإجابة: (B) The relatively rapid development of radio.
الشرح: المحاضرة تتحدث بشكل أساسي عن "التطور السريع نسبياً للراديو".
42. Q: This lecture would probably be given in which of the following courses?
الإجابة: (D) The History of Communications.
الشرح: بالنظر للموضوع (تطور الراديو وتأسيس هيئة الاتصالات)، هذه المحاضرة تناسب مقرر "تاريخ الاتصالات".
43. Q: Which of the following best describes the situation in early radio broadcasting?
الإجابة: (C) The existing radio stations were totally uncontrolled.
الشرح: ذُكر في المحاضرة أن البث الإذاعي في البداية كان "غير خاضع لأي سيطرة أو تنظيم" (totally uncontrolled).
44. Q: What do the initials FCC stand for?
الإجابة: (D) Federal communications commission.
الشرح: ذُكر نصاً أن FCC هي اختصار لـ "مفوضية/هيئة الاتصالات الفيدرالية".
المحادثة الثالثة: جولة في متاحف سميثسونيان (أسئلة 45-50)
I hope you've enjoyed. So far in Washington, D.C. Today we're going on a tour of the Smithsonian. The Smithsonian is actually several museums, each with a different focus, situated together on a mall. These museums in total have more than 60 million items on exhibit. The first Smithsonian museum we'll visit is the Museum of Natural History, which has various types of stuffed animals and exhibits showing the lifestyles of early American Indians and Eskimos. From the Museum of Natural History, we'll go on to the National Air and Space Museum, where we'll see displays that show the development of flight. In this museum, you can see the airplane that Orville Wright used to make his first flight, and the airplane that Charles Lindbergh used to cross the Atlantic. After we visit those two museums as a group, you'll have free time to visit some of the other Smithsonian museums. The Museum of American History, the Smithsonian Arts and Industries Building, and the various art museums located on the Smithsonian Mall. After our trip to the Smithsonian today, we'll go on to the White House and Capitol Building tomorrow.
45. Q: Who is probably giving this talk?
الإجابة: (B) A tour guide.
الشرح: المتحدث يوجه مجموعة سياحية ويشرح لهم خط سير الزيارة، فهو "مرشد سياحي".
46. Q: How many items are on exhibit in the Smithsonian museums?
الإجابة: (D) Millions and millions.
الشرح: ذُكر أن المتاحف تضم أكثر من "60 مليون" قطعة معروضة.
47. Q: According to the passage, which museum has exhibits of early Eskimos?
الإجابة: (B) The Museum of Natural History.
الشرح: متحف التاريخ الطبيعي هو الذي يضم معروضات عن نمط حياة الهنود الأمريكيين الأوائل والإسكيمو.
48. Q: Which museum will they visit as a group?
الإجابة: (D) The National Air and Space Museum.
الشرح: المتحف الوطني للطيران والفضاء هو أحد المتحفين اللذين ستزورهما المجموعة معاً.
49. Q: According to the speaker, when is it possible for the tour members to go (to the other museums)?
الإجابة: (B) After visiting other museums.
الشرح: سيكون لديهم وقت حر لزيارة المتاحف الأخرى "بعد" زيارة المتحفين الأساسيين كفريق.
50. Q: Where will they go tomorrow?
الإجابة: (A) To the White House.
الشرح: في نهاية المحادثة قال المرشد إنهم سيذهبون غداً إلى "البيت الأبيض" ومبنى الكابيتول.
